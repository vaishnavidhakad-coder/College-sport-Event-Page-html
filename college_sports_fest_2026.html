Rules & Regulations

Please adhere to all guidelines to ensure fair and safe competition

  1. Participants must carry their college ID card.
  2. Participants must report on time.
  3. Follow the instructions of the sports coordinator.
  4. Maintain discipline and sportsmanship.
  5. Proper sports equipment must be used.
  6. Respect opponents, teammates, and officials.
